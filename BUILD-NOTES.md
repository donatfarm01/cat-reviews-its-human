# Build notes — Cat Reviews Its Human

The entry is one funny single-line one-star review, written in the voice of a
house cat about its human, under 200 characters. The program holds the review
as a single constant and writes it, unchanged, to the output entry file.

Why this complaint: the judge's brief rewards a specific, recognisable
complaint, a voice that is unmistakably feline, and a turn or sting in the
last few words. The door is the classic cat grievance — the human opens it,
the cat goes out, the human closes it, the cat wants back in — and every cat
owner recognises it at once. The review blames the human for the whole cycle
("He calls this my fault"), which is exactly how a cat measures its human:
by cat standards, the human is the one who got it wrong.

The sting lands at the end: the cat has already knocked the plant over, and
announces it will do it again. The deadpan one-star shape stays intact the
whole way through — a complaint, filed formally, about a door — while the
content goes somewhere only a cat would go.

Revision: the first draft said "his plant". The final draft says "his
favourite plant", one word added, because the sting is sharper when the
plant mattered to the human. Nothing else changed; the line stays a single
line, well under the 200-character gate.
